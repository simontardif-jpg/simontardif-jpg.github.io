Simon Tardif

Doctoral researcher in philosophy at the École normale supérieure (PSL, Paris) and the Université Laval (Québec).

I work at the junction of the phenomenology of history (Heidegger, Schürmann, Blumenberg), French and German philosophical anthropology and the philosophy of the cognitive sciences (4E cognition, generative passages, etc.). My doctoral research, L’Archéologie du visible, proposes an anthropologization of phenomenological description. Drawing on Blumenberg’s transgression of the Anthropologieverbot, on Sloterdijk’s insightful reading of the phenomenal structures of Sein und Zeit, and on Lambros Malafouris’s material engagement theory in cognitive archaeology, it examines the cognitive presuppositions of Husserl’s and Heidegger’s classical methods, and the historical and material conditions, down to the paleo-archaeological record, under which the anthropological phenomenon can be described.

Doctoral project

Research

L’Archéologie du visible : Anthropologisation de la description phénoménologique à l’aune des sciences cognitives

The dissertation examines the historical, material and cognitive foundations of phenomenological description. It looks at the cognitive presuppositions built into the methods of Husserl and Heidegger, and at the cognitive bases of what those methods describe.

The project extends the critical line of German philosophical anthropology (Hans Blumenberg, Peter Sloterdijk) and draws on Lambros Malafouris’s material engagement theory from cognitive archaeology. Its anthropological lens bears on the forms and modes of phenomenological description, and not only on its content. The project thus revisits, from a methodological standpoint, the relation between the empirical and the transcendental. More broadly, I am interested in developing a knowledge of the fine-grained transformations of the structures and schematisms of our experience.

A second line of research concerns the relation between the naturalization of phenomenology and the “phenomenologization” of the cognitive sciences.
